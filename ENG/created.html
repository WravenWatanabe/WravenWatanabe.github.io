The Contact Card

Type: Fine Art

2 x 3.5 inches

The Contact Card is an act of intentionality and an exercise in mindfulness. Before I can offer anyone this channel of connection, I must first expend deliberate effort to produce the card.

These cards may be used to contact me via electronic mail. Each features unique freehand artwork, hand written address, and personal seal stamped in acrylic with self-carved hanko.

Materials:

  • Gmund cardstock, pre-cut: Matt 32, Matt 35, Matt 90
  • Permanent Marker
  • Acrylic Paint
